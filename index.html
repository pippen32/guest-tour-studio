⚠️ The honest note — read this before you pay

Month 1 is seeding. We build your one-sheet, research your show list and send the first waves of pitches — and hosts book weeks out, so bookings normally land in month 2, not month 1. Anyone who promises you interviews in week one is selling you a story. We also never guarantee specific shows or audience sizes — hosts decide who sits in their guest chair. What we publish, and what you can hold us to, is the process and the goals above. That's why every plan carries a 3-month minimum: a tour needs one month to plant and two to play.

This is NOT for you if…

  • You have no book, no business and no real story yet. Hosts book proof, not potential — come back when you have something listeners can act on.
  • You need bookings this week. Month 1 is seeding by design. If your launch is in 10 days, this service is too slow for you, honestly.
  • You want a specific famous show guaranteed. Nobody can sell you that. We pitch hard; hosts decide.
  • You won't show up. We fill the chair — you still have to sit in it, prepared, on time, with a working microphone.

Before you join the tour

When will my first interview actually happen?

Normally in month 2. Month 1 is seeding: one-sheet, research and the first pitch waves. Hosts record weeks ahead, so confirmed dates cluster from month 2 onward — that's the honest timeline, and it's why the minimum is 3 months.

Can you get me on a specific show?

No — and run from anyone who says yes. Hosts choose their guests. We research the right shows, pitch you properly and follow up relentlessly, and we publish our booking goals up front. Specific shows and audience sizes are never guaranteed.

What do you need from me to start?

Your credentials: a book, a business, a course, a practice — something real — plus a headshot, your bio and 30 minutes to agree your interview topics. After that, you only show up to the recordings we book.

How does monthly payment work?

Your first month is paid once through checkout to start the seeding work. The service then continues month to month under the 3-month minimum — there is no surprise auto-subscription promise here; each month is confirmed with you.

I'm launching a book in three months. Is this timed right?

Perfectly, actually. Starting now means the seeding month happens before your launch window, so interviews can land while the book is news. Tell us the launch date in your intake and we pitch around it.

What if my niche is small?

Small niches often book easier — hosts in tight industries are hungry for credible guests, and guest-ready shows fill a median of 21 guest slots a year. If we research your niche and genuinely can't build a viable show list, we'll tell you before month 2 instead of taking your money quietly.
